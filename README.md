# 👕 ReWear AI

### *Redefining Sustainable Fashion with Artificial Intelligence*

An AI-powered sustainable fashion marketplace where users can **buy, sell, swap, and discover pre-owned fashion** through intelligent recommendations, smart pricing, and a seamless modern shopping experience.

---

# 🌍 Vision

The fashion industry is one of the largest contributors to pollution and textile waste worldwide. Millions of wearable clothes end up in landfills every year because sustainable alternatives often feel inconvenient, outdated, or inaccessible.

**ReWear AI** is built to change that.

Our mission is to create a smarter circular fashion ecosystem where people can:

* Extend the life cycle of clothing
* Reduce fashion waste
* Save money through thrifting & swapping
* Discover unique fashion sustainably
* Experience AI-powered personalization

We believe sustainability should feel modern, intelligent, and effortless.
